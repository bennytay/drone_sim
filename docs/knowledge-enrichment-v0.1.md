# Knowledge enrichment v0.1

Knowledge sources retain kind, stable locator, retrieval time, optional publication time and hash, bounded summary, and permitted use. Deployment evidence, manufacturer material, regulations, site rules, and current environmental context may support facts. Engineering references, platform knowledge, and model world knowledge can motivate hypotheses but cannot establish deployment facts.

When fact-eligible claims conflict, deployment evidence takes precedence over external material. Sources are never discarded: hypotheses cite the deployment fact and any knowledge prompt separately.
